Districts, batch 1

Layout drawings for the first three new districts. They show where things stand and how big they are. There are no colours, textures or lighting yet; those come after you approve the layout.

Every drawing is to scale. One city block is 100 m: a 30 m road on two sides and a 62 m lot. The small orange car is the saloon, 1.9 × 4.4 m.

District 1 of 3

Never Lived In

A finished new town where no home has ever switched a light on. Every street lamp works. Tower pairs repeat, every third row of lots is still empty paving, and a lit stadium sits in the middle with nobody in it.

District 2 of 3

Field of Blocks

Every lot is filled with plain concrete slabs. They start at knee height by the road and rise as the ground sinks, until the walls beside you are 8.5 m and the city is gone. Nothing inside is lit except by your own headlight.

District 3 of 3

Office Park

The same low glass office on every lot, at the back, behind a painted car park with nobody parked. Some floors stay lit all night on timers. One building is dark until you come near.

What I need from you

Tell me approve, or name what to change in any drawing: positions, sizes, counts, or anything that should be added or removed.

  • Never Lived In: is a third of the lots empty too many, or right?
  • Field of Blocks: are 5 m lanes tight enough? The car has 0.8 m to spare on each side.
  • Office Park: should the building sit at the back of the lot as drawn, or closer to the road?
