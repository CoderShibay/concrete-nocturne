Districts, batch 2

Layout drawings for the next three districts. They show where things stand and how big they are. There are no colours, textures or lighting yet; those come after you approve the layout.

Every drawing is to scale. One city block is 100 m: a 30 m road on two sides and a 62 m lot. The small orange car is the saloon, 1.9 × 4.4 m and 1.4 m high.

District 1 of 3

Coming Soon

An old four-storey neighbourhood being demolished street by street. The rooms people lived in are cut open to the weather, and a glass tower is advertised over the rubble.

District 2 of 3

Density Wall

Housing so tall and tightly packed that the sky is a strip. Most windows stay lit all night. It is the one district full of people, and you can't reach any of them.

District 3 of 3

The Port

A container terminal that works all night with nobody in sight. Stacks stand like canyon walls, and every crane is moving on its own with a lit, empty cab.

What I need from you

Tell me approve, or name what to change in any drawing. Four decisions are open:

  • Coming Soon: demolition in three bands across the district as drawn, or the three kinds of lot mixed at random?
  • Density Wall: blocks that fill the whole lot as drawn, or smaller blocks with the usual drivable strip around them?
  • Density Wall: windows that stay mostly lit all night, or that go dark like the rest of the city?
  • The Port: include the dock basin and ship, or keep every lot as stacks?
